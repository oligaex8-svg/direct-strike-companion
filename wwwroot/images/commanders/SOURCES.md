# Commander portraits

These 18 small portraits are StarCraft II game artwork, copyright Blizzard Entertainment. They are bundled for this private, local companion application. No claim of an open artwork license is made.

Retrieved 6 October 2026 from the commander navigation on [StarCraft2Coop.com](https://starcraft2coop.com/). Each navigation image has a matching commander name; the site's `hornerportrait.png` is labelled **Han & Horner** and corresponds to the parser's `Horner` value.

Source URL: `https://starcraft2coop.com/images/commanderportraits/{name}portrait.png`.

Names: raynor, kerrigan, artanis, swann, zagara, vorazun, karax, abathur, alarak, nova, stukov, fenix, dehaka, horner, tychus, zeratul, stetmann, mengsk.

The originals are 50 × 50 PNG files, retained without editing. Source URLs, dimensions, sizes, and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `verification/commander-assets.json` at the project root. The app serves only the packaged local copies; opening a player does not contact the image source.
