# What's new

Updates to Direct Strike Companion, explained for players.

## Version 1.1.10 — Smaller app and better battle reports

**4 November 2026**

- **Smaller app:** the executable is about 38% smaller without reducing artwork quality or removing themes.
- **Clearer battle reports:** larger unit icons and a more compact layout make results easier to compare.
- **Summoned units are easier to follow:** when a replay identifies the connection, spawned units appear beneath their summoner with their own contribution bars. This includes recorded chains of units that spawn other units.
- **Damage contributes to Impact:** measured damage dealt and received now count toward a player's team contribution when complete team measurements are available.
- **Better unit recognition:** more original SC2 unit icons and improved tracking of kills from controlled units reduce missing icons and unidentified sources.
- **New screenshot gallery:** see the profile, lobby, player lists and postgame summary in several themes.

Some replay information is still unavailable. Damage for individual units and abilities is not currently measured, and some kills have no identifiable source. The app shows these gaps instead of inventing values.

**How to update:** choose **Check for updates** in the app, then use the update button. Your replay library, settings and player lists are kept. Downloads and updates are public and do not require a GitHub token. If an older version cannot update, download the latest executable and replace the old copy in its existing folder, keeping your data folder.

## Version 1.1.9 — Updates work with downloaded filenames

**27 October 2026**

- Fixed an update failure caused by downloaded filenames, including browser-added numbers such as **(2)**.
- Update failures now provide clearer explanations, including Russian translations.
- The main download is named **DirectStrikeCompanion.exe**. The older download name remains available for compatibility.

If your older copy cannot install this fix automatically, download version 1.1.9 manually and replace the old executable in its existing folder. Keep your data folder.

## Version 1.1.8 — A clearer update window

**27 October 2026**

- Made the update action easier to find and explained that the app restarts afterward.
- Improved English and Russian update messages.
- Clarified that public downloads do not require Git or a GitHub account.

## Version 1.1.7 — More SC2-inspired themes

**27 October 2026**

- Added the latest interface improvements to the downloadable app.
- Expanded the themes with faction and commander artwork, original SC2 equipment and animated details.
- Kept the familiar page layout while giving themes more of their own character.
