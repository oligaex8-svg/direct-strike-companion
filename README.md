# Direct Strike Companion

A Windows companion for StarCraft II **Direct Strike · 3v3 Commanders**. Check your lobby, browse replay statistics and learn more about the players you meet.

[![Download for Windows](assets/download-windows.svg)](https://github.com/oligaex8-svg/direct-strike-companion/releases/latest/download/DirectStrikeCompanion.exe)

**Latest released version: 1.1.9.** The repository is currently private, so downloads and in-app updates require authorized GitHub access.

## What you can do

- **Check the lobby:** see player ratings, recent games and estimated rating gains or losses when enough information is available.
- **Review your games:** browse replays, compare teams and open detailed battle reports.
- **Track familiar players:** save friends, player lists and personal notes.
- **Explore your statistics:** see your most-played commanders, win rates and performance over time.
- **Make it yours:** choose an SC2-inspired theme and use the app in English or Russian.

Your replay library and personal notes stay on your PC.

## Get started

1. Download and open **DirectStrikeCompanion.exe**.
2. Choose your language and check your replay folders and accounts in **Settings**.
3. Select **Scan replays** to import your games.
4. Open **My profile**, **Replays** or **Live lobby** to explore your results.

You do not need Git or a separate .NET installation. If the desktop window cannot open, the app offers **Open in browser**.

## Updating the app

Choose **Check for updates**, then use the update button if a newer version is available. The app restarts after updating and keeps your replay library, settings and player lists.

If an older version cannot update, download the current executable manually and replace the old copy in its existing folder. Keep your data folder.
