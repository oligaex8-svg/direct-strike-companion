# Campaign interiors — local.48

The installed SC2 campaign models, original skin weights, UVs, corner normals and texture references are the source. These are offline Blender reconstructions with explicitly adapted lighting/materials, not SC2 engine captures. The downloaded campaign screenshots in references/ are visual research only and are not shipped as UI backgrounds.

## Reference and construction

Blizzard's [Legacy of the Void campaign preview](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/16668520/legacy-of-the-void-campaign-preview) distinguishes the Bridge/Celestial Array, War Council and Solar Core. The [Hyperion armory article](https://news.blizzard.com/en-gb/article/9988051/lets-show-em-what-we-got) establishes the ship's equipment/upgrades context. Actual reference captures were inspected for gold/crystal architecture, worn metal instruments and living Leviathan tissue; sources.json under references records those image URLs.

The shipped Terran scene joins SM_HyperionBridgeStarmap and SM_HyperionBridgeHolomap at unchanged native world coordinates. The table is physical, while native GLstand ring and MapTwist tracks move the projection. Native LCD flipbook sampling remains visible. It uses 450 samples across the native 37.5-second timeline. Only the named reflection-only/monitor-glass passes received corrective material adaptation; the housing's original textures remain intact. Cubic environment sampling and source UV9 projection are approximated and recorded in render metadata.

The shipped Zerg scene joins the actual campaign EggRoomZerglingEgg and LeviathanFloorTentacle, retaining their native weighted animation. Authored whole-egg scale/translation places it in the living floor; 120 evaluated poses establish contact without an air gap. Six 2.3-second egg cycles and one tentacle cycle run over 13.8 seconds, with the tentacle retimed by 3.50%. Native creep color/height/spec maps ground the assembly with an irregular transparent feather; the ground has no invented movement. Exact staging matrices and timing are in leviathan-nest-data.json.

## Ownership and reproducibility

Export-Campaign48.py and Render-Campaign48.py own source translation. Production rendering uses the exact frozen Render-Campaign48Frozen.py recorded in verification/campaign48-renderer-snapshot.json, allowing prototype-only affine skinning work to proceed separately. Pack-Campaign48.cjs downsamples 2× renders and packs alpha-bounded lossless atlases. campaign-interiors.js/.css place one assembly in the existing sidebar/utility recess and one Settings demonstration. Main content positions and numbers stay fixed. Existing approved Protoss Pylon/grid/warp and Fenix are preserved.

Core architecture/optical-lock assets and the articulating protoss-core-work render are separate Spear-of-Adun research prototypes. The Work prototype preserves full native affine skinning through an exact two-factor SVD palette rather than discarding shear; evaluated Blender-vertex checks passed. It is not automatically substituted for the approved Pylon. Other unsupported material families are explicitly listed in inspection.json.

## Native Armory monitor — local.50 correction

The owner rejected local.49's replacement frames/keycaps; they are removed from production. `SM_HyperionArmoryConsole.m3` now supplies the additional real Terran equipment. Its original `Video_ArmoryScreen.ogv` was extracted from `campaigns/liberty.sc2campaign/base.sc2assets/Assets/Textures/video_armoryscreen.ogv` (SHA-256 `420fce1bb1b32014548932827d4aad665b9f067c0527e748b7f478cd1e8feba0`). The complete 9.766667-second movie is uniformly sampled into all 293 original screen frames at 30 fps, mapped through original UVs and composited through the original materials. The source skeletal Stand is static, 4,167ms; no housing movement is invented. `armory-video-source.json`, `armory-video-sampling.json`, and `wwwroot/themes/terran50/catalog.json` distinguish these timelines and record original hashes. Environment reflection remains an explicitly adapted lighting approximation; this is an offline reconstruction, not an engine capture.

The feature-specific export/render adapters leave the existing frozen station/nest renders intact. Landscape camera fitting retains the full console; every rendered frame passes edge-clearance checks before atlas packing. `terran-instruments.js/.css` owns one monitor in the upper equipment recess and its Settings counterpart; the existing animated holomap remains below it. Main data rectangles do not change. Reduced motion uses a static poster, native blur/modal visibility pauses clocks and forced colours hide decoration. Actual full UI, loop recording and lifecycle evidence: `verification/local50-terran/`. This corrects the requested animation/object scope; it does not approve the archived local.49 art direction.
