# StarCraft I and II notification recordings

Short English and Russian game recordings from StarCraft I and II, Blizzard Entertainment games. Game audio belongs to its respective rights holders. User-facing menus offer actual recordings grouped by language, game, race and alert/unit type; synthesized styles remain only as compatibility keys for older settings.

StarCraft I catalog: [Original StarCraft soundboard](https://github.com/pmaoui/starcraft-soundboard). Six bounded excerpts cover Protoss Nuclear launch, Upgrade complete, Research complete, Additional pylons, Awaiting instructions and Zerg Evolution complete. Source file links, hashes, excerpt start offsets and output metadata are in [the SC1 manifest](../../verification/sc1-audio-assets.json). Combined advisor files were split at their observed silence boundaries; the recordings were normalized to mono PCM for offline playback.

Catalog: [Nuclear Launch Detected — StarCraft II unit sounds](https://www.nuclearlaunchdetected.com/). Individual source URLs, SHA-256 hashes, normalized output hashes and durations are in [the asset manifest](../../verification/sc2-audio-assets.json).

The expanded set adds SC1 Terran/Protoss responses, SC2 alerts for all three races, and Ready/Orders/Attack clips for Marine, Marauder, Ghost, SCV, Siege Tank, Thor, Battlecruiser, Medivac, Zealot, Stalker, High Templar, Dark Templar, Archon, Carrier and Immortal. These action labels identify catalog asset types rather than transcribing the dialogue. [Expanded asset evidence](../../verification/expanded-sound-assets.json) records each source URL, source/output SHA-256, duration and format. [Excluded recordings](../../verification/expanded-sound-exclusions.json) lists full lines longer than the five-second notification limit; they are omitted rather than cut mid-sentence.

[catalog.json](catalog.json) is the canonical preset/label/category/hash registry. [Build-StarCraftLibrary.ps1](../../verification/Build-StarCraftLibrary.ps1) generates the matching browser registry and normalizes additions to 22,050 Hz mono 16-bit PCM using FFmpeg with loudness/headroom adjustment. The original twelve clips and preset keys remain unchanged. Playback adds a brief fade and scales user volume. Clips decode individually on first use, without loading the whole enlarged audio library. No download or FFmpeg installation is needed at runtime. Menu labels and categories support English and Russian. English assets retrieved 8 October 2026.

Russian recordings come from the user's installed StarCraft II build **5.0.17.98310**, specifically `mods/liberty.sc2mod/ruru.sc2assets/LocalizedData/Sounds/`. The contained [extraction helper](../../verification/Extract-RussianSc2Audio.ps1) uses [WoW-Tools/CascLib](https://github.com/WoW-Tools/CascLib) 1.0.23 to read the local CASC archive and select its Russian locale; it writes extracted copies only within verification work files. Neither the extractor nor its NuGet package ships in the application. [Build-RussianSoundLibrary.ps1](../../verification/Build-RussianSoundLibrary.ps1) matches verified asset names to existing notification labels and creates separate `sc2-ru-*` presets. It keeps complete lines within five seconds and normalizes them to the same PCM format and loudness as the English set. [Russian asset evidence](../../verification/russian-sound-assets.json) records archive paths, source/output hashes, format, duration and excluded long lines. The catalog's `language` field denotes the recording's spoken language; omitted legacy fields mean English. Existing English audio and saved selections are preserved. Retrieved 16 October 2026.
